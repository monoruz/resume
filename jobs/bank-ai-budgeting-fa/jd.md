## Assumptions the tailoring rests on

1. The work applies AI to internal financial data — cost and asset budgeting —
   rather than customer-facing product.
2. Data residency matters: an Iranian bank cannot send financial data to external
   LLM providers, so on-premise/self-hosted inference is a hard requirement.
3. Outputs must be auditable and challengeable by internal audit or a regulator;
   a model that cannot explain or reproduce its answer is not deployable.
4. The role sits with engineering rather than quantitative finance; it asks for
   someone who can build and run the systems, not model the budget itself.

Assumption 4 is the one most likely to be wrong — see notes.md.
