# Job research: 2026-10-19

Run date 2026-10-19 (UTC). Freshness cut-off: published on or after **2026-10-12**
(7 days, per `docs/job-search-criteria.md`). Minimums: US$3,000/month full-time,
US$2,000/month part-time.

**Bottom line.** No result is Confirmed-compatible. No posting or company page found this
run states that it accepts people working from Iran, pays in crypto, or accepts
third-party invoices. One relocation vacancy is Conditional. Three remote leads are
Low-priority/unverified. They are strong technical fits, but every compatibility
question is still open.

How this run worked: web search for discovery, then each listing was opened on the
company's own job board where it could be reached (Ashby, Greenhouse and Recruitee
pages, plus Askable's own role page). Some sites (Proxify, Himalayas, web3.career)
block automated reading behind a browser challenge, so claims about them come only
from the board listing and are marked as such.

## Detail

### A1. Askable: Software Engineer, AI Coding Tasks

- **Website / listing:** <https://www.askable.com>, official role page
  <https://www.askable.com/experts/roles/senior-software-engineer>. Also on
  [We Work Remotely](https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/askable-software-engineer-ai-coding-tasks-contract),
  [Remotive](https://remotive.com/remote-jobs/software-development/software-engineer-ai-coding-tasks-2091158).
- **Hiring entity:** Askable Pty Limited, Australia ("Askable Labs, a division of Askable
  Pty Limited", company statement on the role page).
- **Publication date:** 2026-10-16 in the We Work Remotely feed, and 2026-10-17 on
  Remotive and RemoteOK. The official page shows no date.
- **Type:** Contract. "We ask you to commit at least 10 hours a week" (company statement).
- **Compensation:** "US$1,000 for each task that passes our quality review … per accepted
  task, not per hour" (company statement). The part-time minimum of US$2,000/month needs
  two accepted tasks a month. Most tasks "go through more than one review round", so
  monthly pay is **unverified**. Third-party boards list conflicting rates (£750/task,
  $100/h); the official page is used.
- **Iran-based work:** unknown. The page says "Fully remote, from any location" (company
  statement), which under the criteria does not confirm Iran.
- **Payment route:** unknown. "We pay you directly, not through an agency" (company
  statement); the method is not stated.
- **Iran-based team evidence:** none found.
- **Technical fit:** The role needs Python or TypeScript, Docker, Linux, Git, automated
  testing and evaluation design. That matches the 220-case test suite on the optimalist
  project, the CI/CD and container work (homeca, consulting), and the LLM structured-output
  work (yarai, dirichlet). One hard requirement still needs checking: **code merged into a
  public open-source repository** (employer code does not count). master.yaml tags
  open-source work, but the specific merged contribution needs confirming.
- **Hiring urgency:** "Availability … starting now" and "do as many tasks as you want"
  (company statement). The same listing appears on four boards in one week.
- **Reputation:** Trustpilot reviews cover Askable's research-participant payouts, mostly
  positive, with one April 2026 report of a missing payment
  ([Trustpilot](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/www.askable.com), third-party). No reports
  were found about the coding-task contracts specifically, which proves nothing either way.
- **Open questions:** payout method and supported countries; whether a contractor working
  from Iran can sign; typical review turnaround and acceptance rate.
- **Why this rank:** the clearest "any location" wording and stated pay of all the
  results, and a good fit. It stays unverified because neither Iran nor a payment method
  is addressed.

### A2. Tether: AI Harness Engineer (also: Developer Productivity Engineer)

- **Website / listings:** <https://tether.io>, official board <https://tether.recruitee.com>.
  [AI Harness Engineer](https://careers.tether.io/o/ai-harness-engineer-100-remote-worldwide-4)
  was published 2026-10-14. A second fit,
  [Developer Productivity Engineer (100% Remote)](https://careers.tether.io/o/developer-productivity-engineer-100-remote),
  was published 2026-10-15. Dates come from Tether's own Recruitee board.
- **Hiring entity:** Tether Operations Limited, which moved its headquarters from the
  British Virgin Islands to El Salvador in January 2025
  ([heise](https://heise.de/-10241286), third-party report). The employing entity for
  remote staff is not stated.
- **Type / pay:** Full-time permanent; pay undisclosed, so **unverified**.
- **Iran-based work:** unknown. "100% Remote, Worldwide" in the title (company statement).
  None of Tether's 234 open offers mentions Iran. One aggregator lists a Tether role with
  an Iran location, but nothing on Tether's own board matches it, so it is disregarded.
- **Payment route:** unknown. Tether issues USDT, but no posting says salaries are paid
  in it, and the criteria rule out treating crypto activity as evidence.
- **Technical fit:** AI Harness asks for agent harnesses (tool calling, agent loops,
  context management, structured outputs) and evals for non-deterministic systems. That
  matches the LLM agent and structured-output work at yarai and dirichlet. Gaps: "strong
  JavaScript/TypeScript" is partial (TypeScript and Node.js appear in projects, Python is
  primary), and **P2P systems experience is absent** from master.yaml. Developer
  Productivity matches the CI/CD, testing and developer-tooling evidence (homeca,
  consulting) better, but expects strong Node.js.
- **Hiring urgency:** Many engineering roles were published on 12–16 October, several
  re-listed per country (company board). This is active hiring, not verified headcount
  growth.
- **Reputation:** Glassdoor 3.1/5 from about 23–29 reviews. Reviewers praise autonomy and
  criticise "fractured leadership" and "unrealistic deadlines"
  ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Tether-Reviews-E845518.htm), third-party,
  isolated accounts). No nonpayment reports were found.
- **Open questions:** employing entity and contract type for remote hires; whether
  Iran-based staff are possible given Tether's sanctions-compliance practices; pay
  currency.

### B1. Backend Engineer, private-markets operations, Berlin (via Clera)

- **Listing:** <https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/Clera/6f314a0a-ccf8-4792-9b47-75385a90b791>,
  published 2026-10-15 (Ashby).
- **Who is hiring:** Clera Labs Inc. (San Francisco) is a recruiting platform
  ([imprint](https://getclera.com/imprint)). The employer is an undisclosed client: "a
  small engineering team building AI-powered operations infrastructure for private
  markets". All claims here are **recruiter statements**. The client's entity and country
  are unknown, though the role is on-site in Berlin.
- **Type / pay:** Full-time; "€70,000 to €110,000" per year, at least €5,833/month, which
  **meets** the US$3,000 minimum at any plausible exchange rate.
- **Visa:** "Visa sponsorship is available" (recruiter statement). Sponsorship for an
  Iranian applicant is unconfirmed.
- **Technical fit:** The domain matches closely. The role covers fund accounting,
  reporting, **reconciliation** and treasury automation, plus auditability, observability
  and infrastructure as code. That fits the Finestel trading/reconciliation work and the
  SRE background (hamravesh). Stack gap: it requires production **TypeScript + NestJS** and
  **Prisma**. master.yaml shows TypeScript/Node.js/Express, but not NestJS or Prisma.
- **Open questions:** client identity and entity; whether sponsorship covers Iranian
  nationals; whether the NestJS/Prisma requirement is firm.
- **Why Conditional:** sponsorship and pay are stated explicitly. The client and the
  nationality question are unresolved.
